# eCan.ai Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: XX XX, 2026**

Thank you for using eCan.ai ("eCan", "we", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our services.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using our services. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please discontinue use of our services immediately.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide

- **Account Information**: When you register for an eCan account, we may collect your email address, name, nickname, and profile picture.
- **Contact Information**: When you contact customer support or fill out a feedback form, you may provide your contact details and the content of your inquiry.
- **Payment Information**: If you make purchases, payment processing is handled by our third-party payment processor (Stripe). We do not store your payment card details.

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information**: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, IDFA, etc.), screen resolution, CPU model, and memory information.
- **Log Information**: Our servers automatically record access times, pages visited, feature usage, and operation logs when you use eCan services.
- **Location Information**: If you enable location services, we may collect your approximate or precise location.
- **Usage Data**: Features used, interaction patterns, and other behavioral data to improve our services.

### 1.3 Information from Third Parties

- **Social Login**: If you sign in with Google or Apple, we may obtain your public profile information from those platforms.
- **Third-Party SDKs**: See the "Third-Party SDKs" section below.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

1. **Service Delivery**: Creating and managing your account, providing AI-assisted features, and syncing your data.
2. **Product Improvement**: Analyzing usage patterns to optimize features and user experience.
3. **Security and Compliance**: Detecting and preventing security threats, enforcing our Terms of Service, and protecting user rights.
4. **Communications**: Sending product updates, announcements, and service notifications (you can opt out in Settings).
5. **Payment Processing**: Processing your purchases and providing invoices.
6. **Legal Compliance**: Fulfilling our legal obligations.

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), our legal basis for processing your personal data depends on the processing activity:

| Processing Activity | Legal Basis |
|--------------------|-------------|
| Account registration & service delivery | Performance of contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) |

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy:

- **Account Data**: Retained until account deletion + 30 days
- **Usage Logs**: Retained for 12 months
- **Payment Records**: Retained for 7 years (tax compliance)
- **Marketing Data**: Retained until consent withdrawal

When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

## 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

- **Encryption in Transit**: HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data transmissions
- **Encryption at Rest**: AES-256 encryption for stored data
- **Access Controls**: Principle of least privilege; role-based access
- **Security Auditing**: Continuous logging and monitoring
- **Regular Assessments**: Periodic security audits and penetration testing

## 6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

1. **Service Providers**: We share data with trusted vendors who process data on our behalf:
   - Amazon Web Services (AWS) — cloud hosting
   - Stripe — payment processing
   - Google Firebase — authentication and analytics
   - FCM (Firebase Cloud Messaging) — push notifications

2. **Legal Requirements**: When required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights.

3. **Business Transfers**: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred to the acquiring entity.

4. **With Your Consent**: In any other case with your explicit consent.

## 7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

### 7.1 GDPR Rights (EEA Users)

If you are located in the EEA, you have the right to:

| Right | Description | How to Exercise |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| Access | Obtain a copy of your personal data | Settings → Privacy → Export Data |
| Rectification | Correct inaccurate data | Settings → Profile |
| Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten") | Request deletion of your data | Settings → Account → Delete Account |
| Restrict Processing | Limit how we use your data | privacy@ecan.ai |
| Data Portability | Receive your data in a machine-readable format | Settings → Privacy → Export Data |
| Object | Object to processing based on legitimate interests | privacy@ecan.ai |
| Withdraw Consent | Withdraw consent at any time | Settings → Notifications |

### 7.2 CCPA/CPRA Rights (California Residents)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

- **Right to Know**: Know what personal information is collected
- **Right to Delete**: Request deletion of personal information
- **Right to Opt-Out**: Opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell your data)
- **Right to Non-Discrimination**: Equal service regardless of exercising your rights

To exercise your rights, contact us at privacy@ecan.ai or call 1-800-XXX-XXXX.

**Do Not Track**: Our website does not respond to Do Not Track signals.

### 7.3 Response Time

We will respond to your data subject requests within **30 days**. For complex requests, we may extend this by an additional 30 days with prior notice.
